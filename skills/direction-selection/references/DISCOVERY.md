# Evidence for a direction decision

Use a bounded investigation when an evidence need could affect applicability, framing, candidate coverage, ranking, or support for the current commitment. It serves the comparison step that requested it; it does not select a direction.

Before comparison is live, a surrounding caller retains ordinary factual, behavioral, and project-state checks. Standalone use may probe whether a direction problem exists, then return findings to the [applicability router](../SKILL.md#applicability-router). If that remains unresolved, the router hands off without a direction gate.

## Define the investigation

Identify:

- the owning comparison step or standalone applicability question;
- the fact, assumption, reliability concern, coverage gap, or unfamiliar area and why it matters;
- the evidence method and coverage needed, with a checkable stopping condition;
- proportionate cost, delay, risk, and commitment limits.

Domain orientation may precede precise hypotheses. Identify the relevant area and what understanding is needed; predicted discoveries are unnecessary. A seemingly supported favorite still warrants investigation when its deciding evidence lacks a reliable basis.

Choose source research, inspection, tests, representative benchmarks, measurements, or contained prototypes as the question requires. For source work, follow [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md). Prefer a sequence that can resolve the decision with the least justified work, while covering the conditions material to the claim.

## Investigate and return

Perform the defined work and assess it under the main loop's evidence standard. Extend only when remaining gaps or new leads still matter and the expected decision value justifies the added effort. More than one check may be necessary; a cheap but unrepresentative check cannot settle a deciding claim.

Return findings, provenance, limitations, and unresolved needs to the exact owning step when the stopping condition is met or the work cannot be completed. Keep still-applicable results and update only affected comparison. A standalone probe returns to applicability; a live comparison resumes without restarting that router.

If access, time, or cost ends the investigation before its evidence need is met, report the gap. The owner decides whether a blocker or independently supported bounded commitment follows. Distinguish a stable question lacking evidence from structurally unstable uncertainty; difficult retrieval alone does not justify adaptive selection.

Keep experiments within existing authorization and assess reversal after probable dependent work. Exploratory artifacts remain disposable unless an independently passing commitment supports their use. Prevent live exposure, external commitments, or lock-in from silently deciding the unresolved choice.

**Complete when:** the owner receives enough evidence to reassess support, or an explicit limitation and next evidence need. Discovery returns control; it never emits a winner or takes over planning, implementation, persistence, or broader verification.
